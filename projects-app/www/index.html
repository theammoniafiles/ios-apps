Forecast for the quarter = costs already logged in that quarter plus an equal share of each project's remaining budget for every open task due that quarter. Overdue open tasks count toward the current quarter, and open tasks with no due date count toward the quarter of the project's latest open due date (or the current quarter if none). Projected total at completion is estimated from spend so far versus the share of tasks done, so a project burning budget faster than it finishes tasks projects over budget. Projected completion is the latest due date among open tasks. Every forecast is split into Capital and Expense, each run from its own budget and logged costs, so a project can be over on one and under on the other.

Forecasts are calculated on this device from your projects, tasks, and logged costs. Nothing is uploaded or shared — the report only leaves this phone when you download it yourself.
